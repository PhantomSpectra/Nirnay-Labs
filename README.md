# Nirnay Labs
### From Doubt to Decision, with Evidence.

**Nirnay Labs** is a proposed family-centred career decision-support solution developed under **Nirnay Labs** for the Smart India Hackathon.

## Overview

Career decisions are rarely made by students alone. Families often influence educational choices, particularly when considering vocational pathways. Concerns about income, job security, social perception, safety, and long-term growth can make it difficult for students and parents to reach an informed decision.

Nirnay Labs aims to bridge this gap by helping families explore vocational career options through structured guidance and evidence-based information.

## Problem Statement

Students may be interested in vocational education but struggle to gain family support because of uncertainty about career prospects, financial stability, social acceptance, and future opportunities.

Generic career counselling may not fully address these family-level concerns. Nirnay Setu approaches career guidance as a shared decision-making process.

## Proposed Solution

Nirnay Labs aims to help students and families:

- **Identify concerns:** Understand the questions and reservations influencing career decisions.
- **Explore career pathways:** Examine vocational options and their potential opportunities.
- **Evaluate evidence:** Support decisions with credible, relevant information rather than assumptions.
- **Improve communication:** Give students and families a structured way to discuss differing expectations.
- **Support informed decisions:** Encourage choices based on individual interests, practical constraints, and available evidence.

## Core Design Principles

- Family-centred rather than student-only guidance.
- Evidence over hearsay.
- Transparent information and limitations.
- Respect for student aspirations and family concerns.
- Human involvement when a decision requires additional counselling.

## Intended Impact

Nirnay Labs aims to reduce uncertainty around vocational education and help students and families make more informed career decisions.

The objective is not to dictate a career choice. It is to make the decision better informed.
